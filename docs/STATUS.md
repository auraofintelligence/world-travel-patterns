## Done well

- **Entry and activities** for every country and territory outside Australia. Mostly from official sources, but most activity checks still say "confirm in writing" (see below).
- **Health entry** for 235 places, from Smartraveller and WHO's 2022 country list.
- **Seasons**: monthly temperature and rainfall for 241 places (World Bank, 1991 to 2020), with season names, hazard windows and visitor numbers for the Pacific and Smartraveller places.
- **Local law, money and consular help** for every Smartraveller destination, plus consular help for 50 more places from DFAT's list of missions.
- **Births, affection and relationships, psychoactive substances**: every Smartraveller destination, plus UN figures for births and marriage.
- **Languages**: official languages for every country, English capacity (Eurobarometer for the EU, EF index elsewhere), English hotspots, regional languages for 129 regions.
- **Where the law is published**: official law repositories for 250 places; 7 have no online source.

## Not done

Biggest gaps first (revised 1 November 2026).

1. **Activities: confirming in writing.** About seven in ten activity checks still say "confirm in writing": the official pages read so far don't say whether a visitor can do that activity. Each one needs the country's immigration or labour page, or an email to its embassy.
2. **States and regions are mostly empty.** Of 3,518 states and regions, local law covers 12, substances 15, culture 3 and year ahead 9. The rest show "not yet checked". High-visitor regions (Bali, Goa, Phuket, Krabi, Dubrovnik, the South Aegean, Lisbon, Dubai, Kyoto, the Balearics, Lazio, Hawaii and several US states) have been started.
3. **Year ahead.** 35 of 247 countries. 2027 events and rule changes are in for about 15 places (Venice access fee, Rio Carnival, Women's World Cup, Oktoberfest, Expo Belgrade, Tour de France, WYD Seoul, Asian Cup, Lima Pan Am Games, Nashik Kumbh, Cricket World Cup). Most countries still have only elections, or nothing.
4. **Culture and protocol: quality, not coverage.** 244 of 247 (only Australian places are left out on purpose). 206 of the 244 are "ask locally". They are drafted from Smartraveller or FCDO customs text, or say the FCDO page has no customs guidance, and no national etiquette page has been searched for them yet. Next step is each government's or tourism board's own etiquette page.
5. **Visa preparation.** 60 records are still legacy baseline, and about 60 are flagged for fees that no official page states.
6. **Local law.** 35 countries and territories have no local-law record.
7. **Psychoactive substances.** 72 countries and territories have no record.
8. **Permit zones.** 25 zones checked worldwide. Many more exist (national parks, Indigenous lands, restricted areas).
9. **Signals to firm up.** Languages (Wikidata and EF), elections (IFES), holidays (Nager.Date), currency (Wikidata) and several activity findings are signals. Each needs the government's own source.
10. **Daily costs in AUD.** No place has a daily cost yet.
11. **Visitor flow.** Monthly visitor numbers exist only for a few Pacific places and Minjerribah.
12. **Works and purpose.** Not started (Luke's own works, tagged places only).
13. **Place photos.** Reviewed 1 November 2026: 312 approved, 1 pending (Uluṟu, Luke's call). gn, io and us/papahanaumokuakea still need a suitable photo.
14. **Routines.** The weekly and monthly Claude routines in `routines/` are written but not registered to run.

## Known source problems

- Smartraveller's Venezuela page still names the bolívar fuerte (replaced in 2018). The money record now names the bolívar (VES), redenominated on 1 October 2021 (ISO 4217 Amendment 170); Smartraveller's cash notes are kept as read.
- Canada's Controlled Substances Regulations (SOR/2025-242) took effect on 1 October 2026; the psilocybin and MDMA records were re-read against them on 1 November 2026.
- WHO's vaccination list is the 2022 edition. On 1 November 2026 the newest WHO yellow fever country list found was still the November 2022 edition (cdn.who.int countries-with-risk-of-yellow-fever-transmission.pdf), so no newer WHO source was found; some countries last confirmed their rule before 2013.
- Fiji's 2027 public holiday list was reported to contain errors; no revised list from the Fiji Government was found on 1 November 2026 (only third-party calendars).
