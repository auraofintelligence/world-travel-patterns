## Ported from Luke's other repos

- `data/missions/in-australia.json`: 520 foreign embassies, high commissions and consulates in Australia for 168 places, from the reviewed DFAT Protocol snapshot in aura-horn-torus (checked 6 September 2026). The EU delegation is left out because it isn't a place in the tree.
- `data/missions/post-cities.json`: the city and country of 125 Australian posts abroad, from aura-horn-torus's layer (originally Australian-world-travel's abroad page). Used only to tell whether a post sits in the country or next door.
